# Mastercard Cybersecurity Virtual Experience Program (Forage)

**Completion Date:** May 2025  
**Role:** Cybersecurity Analyst – Security Awareness Team  
**Platform:** [Forage](https://www.theforage.com/)

---

## 📘 What I Learned

- **Phishing Threats to Organizations:**  
  Gained a clear understanding of how phishing can compromise employee credentials, data, and business operations.

- **Types of Phishing Attacks:**  
  Learned to recognize various phishing tactics including fake emails, deceptive links, and impersonation.

- **How Mastercard Handles Phishing:**  
  Observed Mastercard’s internal strategies for preventing and mitigating phishing threats through employee education, awareness initiatives, and technical safeguards.

- **Effective Awareness Communication:**  
  Learned how to structure concise, visual-heavy, and actionable cybersecurity awareness presentations for non-technical audiences.

---

## 🛠️ What I Did

- **Email Threat Simulation:**  
  Examined an obvious fake phishing email and restructured it to be more believable, simulating how attackers refine their tactics.

- **Presentation Review:**  
  Studied a real example from Mastercard’s Security Awareness Team, focusing on:
  - Use of visual and contextual examples
  - Clear, concise presentation format
  - Defined action points for identifying phishing emails
